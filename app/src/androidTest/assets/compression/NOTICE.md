# Compression benchmark fixtures

`astronaut.png` is the 512 × 512 photograph of astronaut Eileen Collins from
scikit-image v0.19.3. Its upstream documentation identifies NASA's Great Images
database as the source and states: “No known copyright restrictions, released
into the public domain.” This image is test data, not a product endorsement.

Other fixtures are generated by `tools/benchmark/generate_fixtures.py`. They
contain synthetic pixel patterns, no user photos or personal metadata. Generated
large fixtures exercise decoding/allocation limits; they do not substitute for
high-resolution camera photos when evaluating perceived quality.
