# Privacy policy for Shrink

Effective date: 31 October 2026.

Shrink makes photos smaller on your Android device. It does not send your photos,
file names, editing settings, or compression history to a server. It has no
accounts, advertising, analytics, or remote crash-reporting service, and does not
request Internet access.

## Photos and local storage

You choose photos through Android's photo picker. Shrink accesses the photos you
select; it does not request permission to read your whole photo library. Your
original photos are not overwritten. Shrink creates separate copies and may keep
local results and their history so you can save or share them again. Settings are
stored on the device. Temporary previews are discarded when no longer needed.

You can remove retained copies and their history from Recent photos or Settings.
Clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-private data. Photos already saved
to your gallery remain yours and must be removed through your gallery or file
manager. Shrink excludes its private photos, history, and settings from Android
cloud backup and device-transfer rules.

## Saving and sharing

Save photo writes a separate copy to `Pictures / Image Compressor`. Shared photos
are provided only when you choose Share and select a receiving app. That app
handles the photo according to its own privacy policy. Shrink removes location
and other original EXIF metadata from newly encoded copies; visible information
inside the picture remains visible.

## Contact

For questions or requests about Shrink, contact the publisher through the
[project's support page](https://github.com/SriZan12/Image-Compressor/issues).
Do not post private photos or sensitive documents in public support issues.

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes.
